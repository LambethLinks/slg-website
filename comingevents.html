Meetings November to December 2026

Meetings on the second Tuesday of each month at 8 pm, currently at:
The Bread & Roses, 68 Clapham Manor Street, London SW4 6DZ
(a turning off Clapham High Street - nearest tubes: Clapham North and Clapham Common. The Bread and Roses is opposite the end of Navy Street).

View a map of the area

10 November Police and the Community: Joseph Seddon, the new LGBT Community Liaison Officer for Lambeth and Southwark, talks about his role in making London a safer place for gay people and his experience in the police force as a gay man.  
8 December Discovering Gabriel D'Esty (1897–1968): a theatrical and partly imagined life in the Home Counties. Dr Clifford Williams (author of Courage to Be) describes how he uncovered the fascinating life of a theatrical gay man who found solace in a quiet country cottage, where he lived with his Alsatian dog and tended a lovely garden. This story has been put together using diaries, photographs, newspaper cuttings, other ephemera and stories from a man who knew Gabriel and who visits his grave every Christmas to lay a wreath.
