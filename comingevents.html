Meetings February to August 2025

Meetings on the second Tuesday of each month at 8 pm, currently at:
The Bread & Roses, 68 Clapham Manor Street, London SW4 6DZ
(a turning off Clapham High Street - nearest tubes: Clapham North and Clapham Common. The Bread and Roses is opposite the end of Navy Street).

View a map of the area

11 February Living History Project: Writer/photographer Steve Reeves describes his project  “Before We Were Proud” documenting the life stories of older members of the LGBTQ+ community, particularly those who were young adults before the change in the law in 1967.
11 March LGB Christians: Rev Richard Kirker describes the aims and objectives of this new network, particularly its questioning of current gender identity ideology.
8 April Changing Lives: SLG member Bill Boyd describes through his own personal experiences, how being gay can dramatically change people’s lives .
13 May Revolutionary Acts: Author Jason Okundaye talks about his book Revolutionary Acts: Love and Brotherhood in Black Gay Britain in which he documents the experiences, memories and challenges faced by black gay men from the 1950's to the present day.
10 June Foster Caring: Members of the Croydon Council Fostering team describe their work and their drive to recruit carers from diverse backgrounds, including from the LGBTQ+ community.
8 July Pride- Pits and Perverts: Mike Jackson and Jonathan Blake from the London Lesbians and Gays Support the Miners (LGSM) group tell the story of the Lesbian and Gay support for the miners during their strike of 1984/85.
August No meeting
