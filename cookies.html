Cookies

Cookies are tiny software files that are stored on a computer or mobile device when an individual visits a website. Cookies allow websites to recognise that a user on an individual computer has previously visited the site. The cookies save some information about that user for when they access the site again in the future.

For more information about the different types of cookie, visit the About Cookies website.

SLG, like nearly all websites, uses Google Analytics, a service provided by Google, which uses cookies. This gathers anonymous data of how people are using this site and then provides us with visitor statistics. You can opt out of being tracked by Google Analytics using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser add-on.

Personal data

SLG does not store personal data about visitors to our website.
